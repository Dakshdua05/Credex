## FAQ

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. You get the audit first, then can enter email if you want to save or share the report.

**Is this just a pricing calculator?**
No. It checks plan fit, team size, likely use case, API optimization, and whether discounted credits are a better path than switching tools.

**Will you tell me to switch tools every time?**
No. If your spend is already reasonable, the report says so.

**Can I share the audit publicly?**
Yes. The public report strips email, company, and role while keeping tools, plans, and savings numbers.

**How does Credex fit in?**
When the audit shows meaningful savings, Credex can help eligible companies capture more of that savings through discounted AI infrastructure credits.
